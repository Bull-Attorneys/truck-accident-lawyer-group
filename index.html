NEGLIGENCE

A trucking company can be at fault for their driver under the legal doctrines of Respondeat Superior or Vicarious Liable. This is like when a truck driver runs into the back of your car. The trucking company can also be directly liable for acts of direct negligence like failing to properly hire, train, qualify, retain and supervise their drivers.
